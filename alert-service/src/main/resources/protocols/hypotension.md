# Protocol 4.3: Hypotension (low blood pressure)

DEMO CONTENT for the CareVoice demo of Sunrise Care, a fictional care home. Not clinical guidance.

## When this protocol applies
A hypotension alert is raised when the systolic blood pressure is below 90 mmHg. Low blood pressure increases the
risk of dizziness, falls and fainting.

## Immediate steps
- Help the resident lie down and raise the legs slightly if this is comfortable.
- Recheck the blood pressure and pulse after five minutes lying down.
- Ask about dizziness, light-headedness, recent falls, reduced drinking, vomiting or diarrhoea.
- Do not let the resident stand up alone until the blood pressure has been rechecked.

## When to escalate
- If the resident is drowsy, confused, cold and clammy or loses consciousness, call emergency services.
- If the low blood pressure persists without these signs, inform the physician the same shift.

## Documentation
- Document both readings, the position of the resident, fluid intake and the symptoms you asked about.
- Increase fall precautions until the physician has reviewed the resident.
