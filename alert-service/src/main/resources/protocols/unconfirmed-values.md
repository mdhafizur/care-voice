# Protocol 1.4: Unconfirmed values and alerts that need review

DEMO CONTENT for the CareVoice demo of Sunrise Care, a fictional care home. Not clinical guidance.

## When this protocol applies
An alert can be based on an unconfirmed value: a value that the speech documentation recognised with low confidence or
that the AI fallback extracted. Unconfirmed values are marked NEEDS_REVIEW and must be checked before anyone acts on
them as if they were measured facts.

## Immediate steps
- Treat the alert as a possible emergency until it is ruled out: go to the resident and look at them.
- Measure the vital sign again yourself instead of relying on the recorded value.
- Confirm the value in the documentation app if your measurement agrees, or document the correct value.

## When to escalate
- If your own measurement confirms the alert, follow the protocol for that alert type.
- If your measurement is normal, document it and inform the documenting nurse that the recorded value was wrong.

## Documentation
- Always document who rechecked the value, when, and what the measured value was.
