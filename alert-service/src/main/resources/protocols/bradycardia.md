# Protocol 5.2: Bradycardia (slow pulse, low heart rate)

DEMO CONTENT for the CareVoice demo of Sunrise Care, a fictional care home. Not clinical guidance.

## When this protocol applies
A bradycardia alert is raised when the pulse is below 45 beats per minute (bpm). A slow heart rate can be normal for
some residents, but it can also cause dizziness, fatigue and falls.

## Immediate steps
- Count the pulse again for a full minute and check whether it is regular.
- Measure the blood pressure.
- Ask about dizziness, fatigue, fainting or confusion.
- Check the care plan for a known slow pulse and the resident's usual heart rate.

## When to escalate
- If the resident has fainted, is confused or has low blood pressure, call emergency services.
- If the slow pulse is new and the resident has no symptoms, inform the physician the same shift.

## Documentation
- Document the pulse, its rhythm, the blood pressure and the resident's usual heart rate from the care plan.
