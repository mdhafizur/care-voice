# Protocol 5.1: Tachycardia (fast pulse, high heart rate)

DEMO CONTENT for the CareVoice demo of Sunrise Care, a fictional care home. Not clinical guidance.

## When this protocol applies
A tachycardia alert is raised when the pulse is above 120 beats per minute (bpm). A fast heart rate can be caused by
fever, pain, anxiety, dehydration or a heart rhythm problem.

## Immediate steps
- Let the resident rest for five minutes, then count the pulse again for a full minute.
- Check whether the pulse feels regular or irregular.
- Measure blood pressure and body temperature as well.
- Ask about palpitations, chest pain, shortness of breath or dizziness.

## When to escalate
- If the resident has chest pain, shortness of breath or fainting, call emergency services.
- If the fast pulse persists after rest without these signs, inform the physician within the shift.

## Documentation
- Document the pulse at rest, whether it was regular, the other vital signs and the symptoms you asked about.
